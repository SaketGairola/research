# Video Research Brief: 90–120-second AI-narrated video (for Brag)

Prepared 1 Oct 2026. **This is a content and evidence brief, not a script to render.** Each segment is tagged with the type of material it may use. On-screen captions must show the label and source.

## Evidence-type tags (must appear on screen when relevant)
| Tag | Meaning | On-screen label |
|---|---|---|
| **[AUTHENTIC]** | Genuine 2025 photos or video of prototypes or tastings, recovered and dated | "2025 prototype footage" |
| **[RECOLLECTION]** | Founder's memory, no record | "Founder's account" |
| **[CONCEPT]** | AI-generated or rendered artwork, including cans and the ReSeal animation | "AI-generated concept, not a real product" |
| **[VERIFIED]** | Independently sourced fact | Publisher + year, e.g. "Source: FSSAI" |
| **[ESTIMATE]** | Market-research forecast | "Estimate: Euromonitor via Business Standard, 2026" |

**Current inventory:** no authentic footage has been recovered (as of 1 Oct 2026). Unless it is found, the video must rely on founder-to-camera, concept art (labelled) and data graphics.

## Possible structure (≈ 105 s)
| Time | Segment | Content (facts only) | Allowed material |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–10 s | Hook | "India drinks a fraction of the energy drinks the West does. Euromonitor puts off-trade consumption at 0.4 litres a person, against 2.1 globally." (S14) | [VERIFIED] data graphic |
| 10–25 s | Origin | "In 2025 I worked with a contract manufacturer to make prototypes and tasted them with about 250 students. Blueberry came out on top. But I wasn't happy with the formula, so I stopped." | [RECOLLECTION] founder on camera; [AUTHENTIC] only if recovered |
| 25–45 s | What changed | "In 2026 India's food regulator told the biggest brands to stop calling these drinks 'energy drinks'. They're now caffeinated beverages, capped at 300 mg of caffeine per litre." (S01, S09, S10) | [VERIFIED] headline graphic citing FSSAI and Business Standard |
| 45–65 s | The market reality | Price ladder: Sting ₹20 (blueberry flavour), Red Bull ₹125, Monster Ultra ₹125 (S31, S33, S34). "Cheap and premium are both taken. My question is whether a taste-first adult brand has room." | [VERIFIED] dated price chart |
| 65–85 s | What we'll test | One flavour first; blind tests against the market leaders; paid-purchase pilot in one city; real fruit only if people can taste the difference. | [CONCEPT] can render, labelled; simple test diagrams |
| 85–100 s | Honest economics | "After 40% GST and trade margins, a ₹99 can returns about ₹52 to the brand. That's why the quotes and the pilot decide everything." (S13; model) | [VERIFIED] GST + [ESTIMATE/MODEL] waterfall labelled "model, hypothetical costs" |
| 100–110 s | Future idea | "Later, I want to explore a reseal strap for cans. It's only a concept today." | [CONCEPT] animation, labelled "untested concept" |
| 110–120 s | Close | "I'm looking for a food technologist, a regulatory expert and operators who've scaled beverages." | Founder on camera |

## Claims the narration must NOT make
"Energy drink" as the product name; "boosts performance", "focus", "crash-free", "clean energy", "natural", "real fruit" (unless as a factual juice %), "sugar-free" or "diabetic-safe" (unless formulated and verified); "scientifically dosed"; "launched", "customers", "sales", "repeat buyers"; any percentage from the 2025 tasting; market share; any Starbucks or competition-judge reference; other contestants' funding. See `UNVERIFIED_CLAIMS.md`.

## Production notes
- Show AI art with a persistent corner label. Never animate concept cans on a real shop shelf or in a real person's hand (this implies a real product).
- Put source captions on screen for at least 2 seconds.
- Use an AI voice disclosure if the narration is synthetic.
