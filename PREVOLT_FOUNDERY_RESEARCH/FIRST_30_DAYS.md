## Decision gates
| Gate | Day | Pass condition | If it fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 Regulatory | ~14 | Consultant confirms a sellable name, ingredient list and label for at least one caffeinated SKU | Simplify the formula (caffeine + permitted list only), or pivot to a non-caffeinated sparkling drink |
| G2 Economics | ~21 | At least one written quote gives a positive contribution per can sold at a testable MRP of ₹99 or less (model sheet *Scenarios*) | Raise MRP, change pack or channel, or stop |
| G3 Taste | ~28 | The lead flavour is preferred by ≥55% vs the incumbent in blind tests and scores ≥7/9 liking | Reformulate once, or stop |
| G4 Real-fruit value | ~28 | The real-fruit version is detectably different **and** preferred | Use natural flavour + modest juice |
| G5 Demand signal | ~30 | Deposits or outlet commitments sufficient to sell a 2,000-can batch in 12 weeks | Shrink the pilot or stop |

Only if G1–G3 pass: commit to **one** flavour, a 2,000–5,000-can production run, and a 12-week pilot (`GO_TO_MARKET.md` §4).
