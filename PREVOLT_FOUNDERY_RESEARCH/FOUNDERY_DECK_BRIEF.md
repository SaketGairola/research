## 1. The idea: what would you build?
**Evidence-backed framing:** an adult (18+), taste-led **caffeinated beverage** brand in a 250 ml can. It launches with **one** flavour (chosen by blind testing), declares its caffeine and fruit content factually, and sells first through high-intent outlets in one city.
- Do **not** say "energy drink" or "performance energy drink". FSSAI's 2026 order bars the term, and functional energy claims are not permitted (S09, S10).
- Coconut is presented as a **possible future hydration line**, not part of the caffeinated launch (`PRODUCT_FEASIBILITY.md` §4.3).
- ReSeal is presented as a **future packaging idea under exploration** (`RESEAL_STRAP.md`).
**Exhibit:** product concept card listing what is defined (format, caffeine range 36–75 mg per can under FSSAI's 145–300 mg/L, S01) and what is still to be developed (fruit system, sweetener, shelf life).
**Visual:** if used, label concept artwork "AI-generated concept, not a manufactured product".

## 2. The consumer: who is it for, and what are they trying to achieve?
- Adults 18–30 in one city: students (18+), young professionals, gym-goers. **Hypothesis**, not yet segmented with data.
- Verified context: 62% of medical students in one Delhi college had tried energy drinks; most drank them less than weekly and bought from local shops (S46). Category growth was led by affordability (S14, S15).
- What they are "trying to achieve" (alertness, a pre-workout ritual, taste, social habit) is **unranked in Indian research**. Present it as a question being tested in the founder's interviews.
**Exhibit:** segment table marked "hypothesis", with the 15 interview questions as the method.

## 3. The gap: what is missing from existing alternatives?
**Be careful. Most "obvious" gaps are already filled:** zero-sugar (Monster Ultra, Campa), blueberry (Sting Gold at ₹20), citrus (Campa Citrus Kick), affordability (₹10–₹30) (S16, S33, S34, S36).
**The defensible statement:** "We have not found a mainstream Indian caffeinated drink that leads with declared fruit-juice content and a taste-first, adult positioning in a premium can. Whether that is a gap or simply no demand is what our blind tests and paid-purchase pilot will show."
**Exhibit:** competitor price ladder per 100 ml: Sting ₹8, Campa (reported), Monster Ultra ₹35.7, Red Bull ₹50, 28 BLACK ₹56.8 selling (S31–S35) (`COMPETITOR_ANALYSIS.md` §1).

## 6. The business: how could it become commercially sustainable?
- **Honest headline from the model:** at base assumptions (₹99 MRP, 20% retailer + 8% distributor margin, 40% GST, 10% sampling, 75% sell-through), **every first-batch scenario loses money**. Break-even MRP is about ₹121 (lemon, 5k) to ₹155 (blueberry, 5k) (`UNIT_ECONOMICS.xlsx` → *Breakeven_MRP*). Under favourable but unverified costs, contribution is about ₹20–28 per can at ₹99 (*Best_Case_Check*).
- **What must be true:** written quotes near the favourable end; a taste win that supports ≥ ₹99; high-intent channels with lower trade costs; ≥25% repeat within 30 days.
- **Three flavours at once** needs about ₹13.6 lakh vs about ₹3.9–5.2 lakh for one lemon SKU (*Three_Flavour_vs_Staged*, hypothetical). Recommend **staged**.
**Exhibit:** waterfall from ₹99 MRP to GST, retailer, distributor, net realisation (₹52) and cost (hypothetical). Show labels on every bar.
